# 🧭 NutOS Team Structure
*Rodent, Inc. | SquirrelLabs Technologies*

---

## Organization Model

NutOS is developed by **Rodent, Inc.** as an operating division of **SquirrelLabs Technologies**.

### Core Delivery Teams

1. **Kernel Team**
   - NutKernel, boot, low-level performance, platform enablement
2. **NutShell Compatibility Team**
   - LinuxRun, WinBridge, DroidShell, compatibility telemetry (opt-in only)
3. **Acorn Desktop Team**
   - Shell, compositor, design system, accessibility
4. **Security Team**
   - NutCage, hardening, incident response, threat modeling
5. **Hardware Partnerships Team**
   - Certification labs, OEM relationships, driver readiness
6. **Developer Relations**
   - SDK/docs, community contribution flow, ecosystem outreach

---

## Shared Functions

- **Program Management:** roadmap orchestration and dependency tracking
- **Quality Engineering:** cross-team integration and release validation
- **Release Engineering:** build, signing, update pipeline operations
- **Design & Research:** UX consistency, usability studies, and a11y audits

---

## Governance Cadence

- Weekly team syncs by function
- Biweekly cross-functional architecture review
- Monthly roadmap and risk review
- Quarterly planning and objective reset

---

## Decision Framework

- Security and data ownership requirements are non-negotiable gates.
- User-facing complexity must be justified by measurable value.
- Backward compatibility tradeoffs require explicit release-note disclosure.
